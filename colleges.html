FirstDorm’s Offering to Colleges

New Hostel Development : Building for Tomorrow

We design and construct cutting-edge student residences from the ground up — spaces that inspire, comfort, and prepare students for the future.

What we deliver:

  • Contemporary architecture and design
  • Sustainable, net-zero energy buildings
  • Tech-enabled infrastructure
  • Optimal space utilization
  • Future-ready amenities

Retrofit & Upgrade Existing Hostels : Breathing New Life into Old Hostels

Most existing hostels need transformation, not replacement. We specialize in converting outdated facilities into modern, desirable living spaces.

Our retrofit process:

  • Structural assessment
  • Modern redesign
  • Complete furnishing/furbishing
  • Technology integration
  • Sustainability upgrades

Captive Off-Campus Hostels

For institutions needing additional capacity, we acquire land and build dedicated student residences near your campus — ensuring quality housing without campus constraints.

Operations & Management : Invisible when things run smoothly. Present when it matters.

Professional management that enhances student life while freeing your team.

We manage:

  • 24/7 security and access control
  • Regular housekeeping
  • Maintenance and repairs
  • Dining and meal services
  • Student engagement programs
  • Wellness and mental health support
  • Emergency response

How the Partnership Works

We Invest

FirstDorm funds the design, construction, upgrading, furnishing, and long‑term management of student housing. For captive off‑campus hostels, we also acquire the land.

You Provide

  • Land or existing hostel assets (for on‑campus projects)
  • Student demand assurance (typically ~90% occupancy for on‑campus and captive off‑campus models)

Together, We Win
